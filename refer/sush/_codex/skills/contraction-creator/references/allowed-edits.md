# NA800 Modules and Allowed Edits
 
The two assets are the authoritative layout templates. Read them as the fixed `M0`–`M8` modules described in `module-layout.md`. Generated scripts must preserve module order, headings, timer variables, dynamic-call form, and gathering/saving blocks.
 
Parameters and paths, the `M3` dynamic manifest, physical operators and currents, Registry data, time and momentum constants, output axes, and saved metadata may be changed. A structural change must include its physical reason and a before/after difference.
 
The 2pt asset uses `Scatter`, and the 3pt asset uses `TScatter`; do not exchange these paths without repeating the ownership and gathering review.
