The plan still excludes all 2pt measurements and writes only the final txt output in the run directory.

The main physics correction is to stop presenting this as a controlled decay calculation. On the fixed `C24P29` ensemble, with isotropic spacing `a \approx 0.1052 fm` and the provided bare bottom mass `am_b=1.5`, the bottom line is not backed by a validated heavy-quark formulation in the plan. Rather than inventing unsupported RHQ/Fermilab tuning data or changing the fixed ensemble block, the revised plan states explicitly that the output is a bare correlator useful for testing the sequential-source pipeline, not a physical form-factor or decay-amplitude result.

The propagator specification is also corrected. The light propagator is a standard forward point-source solve and is reused for the `u` and `d` spectator lines by isospin symmetry. The bottom propagator is a forward point-source solve from the origin and is identified as the current-side incoming heavy line. The charm sequential propagator is no longer mislabeled as a point source at `t=8`; it is now described correctly as a sink-constructed sequential object built from the `Lambda_c` sink block at zero momentum and `tseq=8`, followed by the usual two-dagger `gamma_5` construction and a charm inversion.

The sink block uses the `Lambda_c` operator with `C gamma_5` diquark structure and the positive-parity projector, the sequential inversion is performed in the charm channel, and the final contraction is between the daggered charm sequential propagator, `gamma_x`, and the forward bottom propagator. The current is explicitly marked as bare and unrenormalized, and the scope statement now makes clear that no current matching, no `O(a)` improvement, and no 2pt normalization program are included.
